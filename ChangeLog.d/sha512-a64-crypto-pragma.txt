Bugfix
   * Fix a build failure with GCC 13 or later on AArch64 when
     MBEDTLS_SHA512_USE_A64_CRYPTO_IF_PRESENT or
     MBEDTLS_SHA512_USE_A64_CRYPTO_ONLY is enabled and the target CPU has
     extensions that Armv8.2-A does not include, such as -mcpu=cortex-a76.
     The sha512 module then failed to inline always_inline functions such as
     a fortified memset().
