Security
   * Fix a TLS 1.2 server accepting a session ticket that was issued for a
     TLS 1.3 session. The session was resumed with an all-zero master secret,
     so anyone holding the ticket could complete the abbreviated handshake and
     derive the connection keys from public handshake values. TLS 1.3 tickets
     appear in clear in the pre_shared_key extension of a TLS 1.3 resumption
     ClientHello. This affects servers that enable both TLS 1.2 and TLS 1.3
     and session tickets.
