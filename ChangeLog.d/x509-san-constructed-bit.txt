Bugfix
   * The subjectAltName parser no longer ignores the primitive/constructed
     bit of a GeneralName tag. Previously an entry tagged 0xA2 (constructed
     [2]) was parsed as a dNSName and used for hostname verification.
     Certificates and CSRs with a GeneralName in the wrong form are now
     rejected with MBEDTLS_ERR_ASN1_UNEXPECTED_TAG. Fixes #11024.
