Bugfix
   * Fix a TLS 1.3 handshake failure on the server side when the client
     sends early data with a ticket that the server cannot use, for example
     after a ticket key rotation. The server should skip the early data up to
     its configured maximum and continue with a 1-RTT handshake, but it used
     a limit of zero and aborted the handshake with
     MBEDTLS_ERR_SSL_UNEXPECTED_MESSAGE.
